# Android 1.4.8 is the reference; every difference is a recorded gap

"Same as Android" means Android IPVIO Stream 1.4.8 (`f4a0360`) until a parity sync moves the reference forward. Anything the webOS app cannot or does not do is listed in `docs/PARITY.md` with a reason and a status (removed, replaced, partial, pending); nothing is dropped silently, and adding a gap needs the maintainer's approval. The Donate screen is a deliberate gap by maintainer decision, not a platform limit.
